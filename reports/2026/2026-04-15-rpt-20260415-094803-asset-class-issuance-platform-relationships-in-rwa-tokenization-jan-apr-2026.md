## Executive Summary

The clearest structural pattern in this period is breadth on the asset-class side rather than dominance by a single issuance platform. Stablecoin, funds, debt, and equity each appeared across wide issuance-platform sets, but they did so through different mechanisms: stablecoins clustered around payment, treasury, custody, and minting rails; funds spread across regulated fund-tokenization and collateral frameworks; debt paired with specialized bond, deposit, and structured-credit platforms; and equity showed the strongest concentrated platform relationships, especially around Securitize, xStocks, OTCM Protocol, and Ondo Global Markets. Real estate remained materially present but more fragmented across specialized and regulated issuance pathways, while natural resources and other alternative assets expanded through narrower, asset-specific platforms.

### Stablecoin shows the broadest issuance-platform coverage, with specialization concentrated in payment, treasury, and minting infrastructure

**Signal:** Stablecoin is the broadest asset-class segment in the dataset, appearing across the widest range of issuance-platform counterparts. Within that broad coverage, the strongest recurring structure is not generic issuance but specialized rails for treasury transfers, institutional minting, cross-border settlement, and branded or application-specific stablecoin deployment.

**Evidence:** Circle-related infrastructure appears repeatedly in this role. Circle Mint supported internal treasury transfers, was validated through a SOC 1 Type 2 examination, and was also linked to institutional integrations involving ClearBank Europe and Movantis through Circle Payments Network connectivity. Other stablecoin platforms show narrower but clear specialization: Ripple-linked initiatives focused on institutional payment infrastructure, Canton Network was used for privacy-preserving payment and settlement use cases, BitGo Mint enabled native institutional minting and redemption, and PYUSDx was positioned as a platform for issuing PYUSD-backed branded stablecoins. Stablecoin expansion also extended to regulated public-chain issuance and niche use cases, including SG-FORGE’s EUR CoinVertible on XRP Ledger, AllUnity’s CHFAU on Ethereum, and use-case-specific issuance through Bridge and STBL-related architecture.

**Strategic Implication:** Stablecoin breadth reflects a market structure in which issuance platforms increasingly function as operational money infrastructure rather than simple token launch venues. The segment’s platform diversity suggests that payment, treasury, and redemption workflows are becoming one of the most mature relationship patterns in tokenized asset markets.
