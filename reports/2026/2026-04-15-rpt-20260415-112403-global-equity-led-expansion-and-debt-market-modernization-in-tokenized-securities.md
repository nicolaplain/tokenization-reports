## Executive Summary

Equity remained the leading asset-class development in the filtered scope, with the strongest evidence concentrated around regulated trading venues, tokenized stock distribution networks, and public-market integration mechanisms that extend equity trading toward continuous, onchain market structure. Debt remained a material secondary segment, but its development pattern was more concentrated in bond issuance, structured credit conversion, and settlement modernization rather than broad retail-style trading access. Across both asset classes, repeated evidence points to compliance infrastructure, transfer-agent models, identity and disclosure layers, and regulated issuance frameworks as the operating backbone through which tokenized securities are being scaled.

## Main Analytical Sections

### Regulated Onchain Equity Trading Infrastructure Became the Core Growth Mechanism

**Signal:** The dominant equity signal is not just more tokenized stock products, but the buildout of regulated or regulation-aware trading infrastructure that re-creates core equity market functions onchain. Repeated evidence shows platforms pushing toward 24/7 or extended-hours trading, unified order books, instant or faster settlement, and integration of tokenized equities into institutional market structure.

**Evidence:** NYSE appeared repeatedly in stories tied to blockchain-based venues for tokenized stocks and ETFs, including a dedicated 24/7 trading venue and a memorandum with Securitize to advance a tokenized securities platform. Nasdaq also featured prominently through SEC-approved tokenized securities proposals and pilot-related developments tied to continuous access and integration of tokenized shares with more traditional trading environments. Figure’s OPEN network, SBI Holdings and Startale’s Layer-1 for tokenized stocks, BEX Global’s 24/7 exchange positioning, and France’s new onchain stock exchange all reinforce the same mechanism: equity tokenization is increasingly being built around venue infrastructure rather than isolated single-asset launches.

**Strategic Implication:** This suggests that equity tokenization is advancing through market-structure replication and extension, not only through issuance experiments. The strongest signal in the selected scope is therefore the institutionalization of tokenized equity trading rails.

**Key Facts:**
- NYSE was reported as building a venue for 24/7 trading of tokenized stocks and ETFs.
- Nasdaq-related developments included SEC-approved tokenized securities trading structures.
- Figure launched the On-Chain Public Equity Network (OPEN) for blockchain-registered public equity trading.
- SBI Holdings and Startale Group launched a Layer-1 platform dedicated to tokenized stock trading.
- France’s new stock exchange was reported taking a company public onchain.

### Debt Tokenization Centered on Digital Bond Issuance and Full-Lifecycle Fixed-Income Infrastructure

**Signal:** Debt remained a material secondary segment, but its clearest recurring mechanism was digitally native bond issuance and infrastructure that supports issuance, settlement, and lifecycle management. Unlike equity, the debt signal was less about broad retail-style distribution and more about institutional fixed-income modernization.

**Evidence:** Citi issued a digitally native structured note on Euroclear’s D-FMI DLT platform, while Canada completed a tokenized bond under Project Samara and earlier bond issuance experiments involving the Bank of Canada, RBC, and TD were reported as successfully completed. Additional issuance-led examples include SBI Holdings’ blockchain-based security token bonds, T-RIZE’s private credit digital bond programme on Canton Network, and the UK Treasury’s selection of HSBC Orion for a digital gilt pilot.

**Strategic Implication:** Debt tokenization is progressing through institutional issuance stacks and post-trade efficiency gains. This makes debt structurally important even though it trails equity in overall filtered prominence.

### Compliance, Transfer-Agent, Identity, and Licensing Layers Recurred as the Common Scaling Backbone

**Signal:** Across both equity and debt, the most consistent cross-asset pattern was the construction of compliance and operational layers that make tokenized securities usable within regulated market settings. These layers include transfer-agent approvals, identity and accreditation systems, licensing frameworks, disclosure standards, and regulated tokenization subsidiaries.

**Evidence:** T7X secured SEC approval to operate as a licensed transfer agent for digital securities, while Global Settlement launched GSX Identity by combining accreditation and tokenized security verification capabilities. OTCM Protocol reported achieving an SEC Category 1 tokenized securities framework, and North Capital launched a tokenization subsidiary serving debt and equity markets. Hong Kong SFC finalized a regulatory structure for tokenised securities, Finsec received a licence for an asset tokenisation market, and SEC-related guidance on issuer-approved tokenized stocks versus synthetic products appeared alongside investor-rights and disclosure discussions. These examples show that infrastructure buildout is being paired with governance and compliance architecture rather than treated as separate from product growth.

**Strategic Implication:** The market’s expansion is being conditioned by operational legitimacy. The repeated presence of compliance layers indicates that scaling tokenized securities increasingly depends on regulated process integration, not just token issuance technology.
