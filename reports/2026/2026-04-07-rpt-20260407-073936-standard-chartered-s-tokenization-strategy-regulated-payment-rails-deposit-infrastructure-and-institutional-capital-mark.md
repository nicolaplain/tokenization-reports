## Executive Summary

Standard Chartered’s activity in the period is defined by a regulated, infrastructure-led tokenization strategy rather than a single product push. The strongest pattern is its use of partnerships, licensing pathways, and banking-grade operating roles to build payment and settlement rails around tokenized deposits and stablecoin-linked services, while also extending into digitally native capital markets products and non-stablecoin tokenization opportunities. Across the evidence, the bank appears less as a standalone issuer and more as an institutional integrator connecting custody, compliance, treasury workflows, cross-border payments, and tokenized investment products.
