## Executive Summary

Stablecoin is the dominant asset-class anchor in this scope, accounting for 77% of classified occurrences and appearing across a much broader set of company counterparts than Cash. The strongest relationship pattern is not a single issuer story but a concentration of Stablecoin pairings across fintech / PSP / infrastructure firms, banks, crypto exchanges, asset managers, tokenization platforms, and custodians, with especially visible company pairings around Coinbase, Mastercard, JPMorgan, BitGo, and Stripe. By contrast, Cash is a smaller but still material segment whose company structure is more concentrated around bank- and asset-manager-led tokenized deposits, tokenized cash settlement, and money-market or Treasury-linked cash instruments, with BNY Mellon the most visible company counterpart in that segment. The comparison shows two distinct relationship structures: Stablecoin maps to broader payment, issuance, custody, and interoperability infrastructure, while Cash maps more narrowly to institutional settlement and tokenized collateral frameworks.
