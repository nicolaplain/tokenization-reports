## Executive Summary

Visa leads the selected scope not just by occurrence share, but by a clearer and more integrated operating pattern: repeated expansion of stablecoin settlement rails, broader card-linked on-chain enablement, advisory support, and a visible governance role in blockchain-based payment infrastructure. Mastercard remains material and active, but its pattern is more partnership- and transaction-led, centered on acquisitions, pilot programs, and regional collaborations that extend tokenized payment and stablecoin use cases rather than demonstrating the same level of end-to-end settlement integration. The comparison therefore points to a structural contrast between Visa’s network-embedded modernization of money movement and Mastercard’s more diversified expansion through external infrastructure, pilots, and localized market linkages.
