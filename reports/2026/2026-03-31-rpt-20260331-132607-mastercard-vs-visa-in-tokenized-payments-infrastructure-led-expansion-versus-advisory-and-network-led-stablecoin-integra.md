## Executive Summary

Mastercard appears slightly ahead of Visa in the selected company scope, and the underlying evidence supports that lead through a broader concentration of infrastructure-building moves: acquisitions of crypto-native payment infrastructure, participation in digital money pilots, expansion of stablecoin settlement rails, and enterprise platform integrations linking on-chain and fiat systems. Visa remains a material peer, but its visible pattern is more centered on extending stablecoin settlement across its existing network, launching advisory capabilities for institutional adoption, and pairing payment integration with research and privacy-preserving blockchain roles. The clearest comparison is therefore not simply that both are active in digital assets, but that Mastercard’s activity skews toward ownership and control of settlement infrastructure, while Visa’s skews toward network enablement, client onboarding, and secure deployment of tokenized payment capabilities.
