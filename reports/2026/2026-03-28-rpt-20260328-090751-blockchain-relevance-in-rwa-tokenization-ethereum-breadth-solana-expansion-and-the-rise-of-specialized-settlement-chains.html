Blockchain Relevance in RWA Tokenization: Ethereum Breadth, Solana Expansion, and the Rise of Specialized Settlement Chains

Executive Summary

The March 2026 RWA tokenization landscape shows a clear structural contrast across blockchains rather than a single dominant model. Ethereum remains the broadest institutional base layer, appearing across regulated funds, tokenized commodities, stablecoin vaults, and yield-linked products, while Base increasingly extends that footprint through lower-friction institutional fund and structured-note deployments. Solana stands out as the strongest high-growth alternative, combining dense tokenized-equity expansion with enterprise distribution and niche asset tokenization. In parallel, XRP Ledger, Avalanche, Bitcoin-native stacks, and a wider group of payment- and compliance-oriented networks are relevant through narrower but distinct mechanisms such as cross-border settlement, real estate tokenization, native Bitcoin issuance rails, and regulated stablecoin infrastructure. The key market comparison is therefore breadth versus specialization: Ethereum-led ecosystems support the widest product diversity, while other chains gain relevance through more concentrated roles in payments, settlement, compliance, or asset-specific tokenization.
