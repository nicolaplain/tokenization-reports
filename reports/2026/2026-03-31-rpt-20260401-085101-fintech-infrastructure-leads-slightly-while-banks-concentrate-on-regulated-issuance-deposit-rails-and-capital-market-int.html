Executive Summary

Fintech / PSP / Infrastructure appears as the leading company-class segment in this scope, and the evidence shows that its edge comes from breadth across settlement rails, compliance tooling, custody infrastructure, cross-chain connectivity, and payment distribution networks. By contrast, banks remain nearly as prominent but are more concentrated around regulated tokenized deposits, stablecoins, custody, bond issuance, collateral mobility, and integration with existing capital-markets and payment frameworks.
